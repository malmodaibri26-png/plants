# Planto 🌱

Planto is an iOS app that helps you take care of your plants. Add your plants in one place and get a daily reminder to water them.

## Main Features
- Add a plant reminder with name, room, light, watering days, and water amount
- See all your plants in one list, grouped with their room and care details
- Mark a plant as watered with a tappable circle
- Track your progress for the day with an animated progress bar
- Swipe a plant to delete it
- Tap a plant to edit it or delete its reminder
- Friendly empty state and "All Done" screen
- Daily local notification to remind you to water your plants
- Your plants are saved on the device

## Technologies Used
- Swift and SwiftUI
- MVVM architecture
- UserNotifications (local notifications)
- UserDefaults for saving data
- Xcode
